HR-Attrition-Analytics: Workforce Intelligence System

Engineered an end-to-end predictive workforce analytics system analyzing 1,470 IBM employee records across 35 features. Identified critical turnover drivers (16.12% baseline attrition, Overtime 3x risk), built machine learning models (~85% accuracy), and deployed an interactive Streamlit dashboard.

  • Synthesized 22 actionable business insights and 8 strategic HR retention recommendations for corporate leadership.
